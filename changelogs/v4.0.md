### What's new

**More detail on every company** — Needlstack now pulls in industry classification codes, fiscal year timing, entity type, country of incorporation, and IPO dates directly from SEC filings. You'll also see security identifiers (FIGI) from OpenFIGI, which helps ensure we're tracking the right instrument even when tickers change.

**Better ownership and short-selling data** — Each company's profile now includes short interest, short ratio, and the percentage held by insiders vs. institutions. This gives you a clearer picture of who owns what and where the crowded trades might be.

**Expanded company profiles** — CEO name, phone number, reporting currency, and accounting standard are now available for every company we track.

**Email updates (like this one!)** — You're receiving this because you're on the Needlstack update list. When we ship something meaningful, you'll get an email. Reply to any update with feedback — it gets read, categorized, and factored into what we build next.

### What to try

1. **Visit [needlstack.com](https://needlstack.com)** and search for any stock or ETF
2. **Check the Metrics tab** — you should see new data points like short interest and insider ownership percentage
3. **Click through the Ownership tab** — institutional holder data is now richer
4. **Reply to this email** with anything — a bug you notice, a feature you want, or just "looks good." All replies are tracked and reviewed.
